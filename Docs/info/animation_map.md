### Lily


**Lily_CheckEarpiece:** Rare idle animation that plays at random. Who knows what comms she's receiving...

**Lily_Idle:** Default animation. It's a windy day!

**Lily_Sprint:** Sprint animation, plays when moving (non-zero horizontal input) while grounded. Blink and you'll miss her--

**Lily_SprintTransition:** Idle-to-Sprint transition, plays when she starts sprinting!
